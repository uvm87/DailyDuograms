Daily Duograms is a free daily trivia game published by UVM87 Games. By playing, you agree to the terms below.

What the game is

Daily Duograms presents one shared puzzle per day, per sport. Players guess real professional athletes based on their initials within a time limit. The game is free to play, requires no account or sign-up, and shows no advertisements.

No official affiliation

Daily Duograms is an independent, fan-made game created by UVM87 Games. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Major League Baseball (MLB), the National Football League (NFL), the National Basketball Association (NBA), the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA), the National Hockey League (NHL), or any team, player, or league mentioned in the game. All team names, league names, and player names referenced are used for informational and identification purposes only, under fair use, as factual data about real public figures and organizations.

What data we collect

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how the game is used. This collects anonymous, aggregate information such as:

  • How many people visit the game, and roughly when
  • Which sport is played, and whether a puzzle was started, resumed, or finished
  • Final scores, in aggregate (not tied to your name or identity)
  • General technical information such as browser type and approximate region, as standard with most analytics tools

We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. We have no login system and no way to identify who, specifically, is playing.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses small text files ("cookies") stored in your browser to help distinguish visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the game will still function normally without them, though usage data may be less accurate.

Where your game progress is stored

Your daily progress (which slots you've solved, your score, and time remaining) is stored only in your own browser's local storage. It is never sent to us or to any server — we have no access to it, and it stays only on your device. Clearing your browser data will erase this progress.

Children's privacy

Daily Duograms does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the game requires no account or personal information of any kind to play, none is knowingly gathered from any player, regardless of age.
